📋 First-Week Orientation Checklist

Make sure you complete these steps in your first 7 days on campus:

  • Document Verification: Submit 10th/12th marksheets, Migration/TC, and allotment letter at Admin Block.
  • Biometric & ID Card: Get your official Smart ID Card photographed at the Student Affairs Center.
  • Campus Wi-Fi & Email: Receive your institutional email (@college.edu) and campus-wide Wi-Fi credentials.
  • LMS Portal: Log in to the Course Portal to access semester timetables and lecture slides.
  • Library Registration: Activate your library RFID tag for borrowing books and digital journals.
💡 Senior Advice: Make at least 5 digital backups and 4 hard copies of all your original documents before arriving on campus!

📈 Understanding CGPA & Credits

College uses a 10-point scale (Cumulative Grade Point Average). Each subject is weighted by credits (typically 3 to 4 credits for theory + lab):

  • O Grade (10 pts): 90% and above
  • A+ Grade (9 pts): 80% - 89%
  • A Grade (8 pts): 70% - 79%
  • B+ Grade (7 pts): 60% - 69%
  • F Grade (0 pts): Below 40% (Fail / Arrear)
⚠️ The Strict 75% Attendance Rule: You must maintain 75% minimum attendance in every course. Falling below 75% results in exam detention.

📝 Evaluation Breakdown

40% Internal Assessment (2 mid-term exams, quizzes, assignments) + 60% End-Semester Exam.

🧳 Must-Pack Essentials for Freshers

  • Bedding: Fitted mattress cover, 2 bedsheets, pillow, light blanket.
  • Electricals: 1 surge-protected power extension board (min 3-4 plugs).
  • Bathroom: Bucket, mug, toiletry kit, laundry bag, clothes hangers.
  • Health: Personal medical kit (pain relief, cold meds, bandages, thermometer).
  • Security: 2 solid brass padlocks with spare keys.

🕒 Hostel Rules & Timings

Hostel gates close at 9:00 PM on weekdays and 9:30 PM on weekends. Weekend night-outs require an approved digital pass via the student hostel app.

🛡️ Zero-Tolerance Anti-Ragging Policy: Ragging in any form (verbal, mental, or physical) is strictly prohibited by law.
